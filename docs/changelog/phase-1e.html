Phase 1e – closing the definition of done

Branch phase-1e-done. Completed 2026-10-12. Not in the original roadmap: the owner asked for the open lines of the MVP's definition of done to be closed before Phase 2. After this phase 16 of its 20 lines are met and 4 partly (after Phase 1d: 12, 7 and 1 not met).

On the owner's desktop, at the owner's request, Aetherius was applied to Ghostty, GTK 4 apps and the top bar on 2026-10-12, after a separate copy of the files was made (D45). The Shell's log shows it loaded the theme.

Added

  • The app keeps its own window in stock colours. Whatever theme is applied to GTK apps, Velatura's window shows libadwaita's colours and the system accent, in light and dark, so an unreadable theme cannot hide the way back. The stock values are read from libadwaita by a script and kept as a table.
  • Every sentence can be translated. The sentences shared by the command and the app (the change list, reasons, hints, failures) now go through the same lookup as the app's own text, with numbered placeholders so a translation can reorder them. The template has 174 strings.
  • "Set up, but not taking effect" in velatura status, which now lists every part with its state, from the same definition the app uses.
  • Other theming tools before the first apply: listed in the app's confirmation and by the command.
  • GNOME 47 to 51 in the selector check. The stock stylesheet of each version was taken from its Fedora package; all 17 selectors Velatura overrides exist in all five.
  • CI on two GNOME versions. The gate runs on Fedora 44 (GNOME 50) and Fedora 45 (GNOME 51, libadwaita 1.10), and now includes the app flow and the colour probe, run in a headless compositor.
  • Renders for accessibility: scripts/gui-shots.sh can render at 200% text size and in High Contrast.

Fixed

  • Tests depended on what the machine had installed. A system-wide User Themes or adw-gtk3 changed the outcome of two tests. The tests now hide the system's shared data.

What only a person at the real session can close

  • The lock screen: what the top bar looks like on lock and after unlock with a theme applied.
